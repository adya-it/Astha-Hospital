Faith is what patients bring. Care is how we answer it.

When someone walks through our doors, they are often frightened, tired or worried for a loved one. Since 2001, Astha Hospital has been the place families in Sultanpur turn to, and we try to deserve that trust every day.

From a mother's first check-up to a broken bone, our doctors and nurses work as one team under one roof.
